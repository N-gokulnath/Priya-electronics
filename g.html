Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions regarding repairs and evaluations? Check out our quick answers.

What is your turnaround duration for initial TV diagnostics?
We aim to conclude standard circuit diagnostics and display evaluations within 12 to 24 hours of device arrival at our shop. Once the physical check is done, we contact you immediately with a complete cost estimation.
Do you offer home-pickup services or on-site screen replacement?
For bracket mounting and software configurations, we support on-site home appointments in Gingee. However, core hardware operations like panel replacement and motherboard micro-soldering must be conducted inside our dust-free shop lab containing diagnostic devices.
Do you provide a warranty on replaced hardware components?
Yes, absolutely! All genuine parts, power boards, and LED backlight array assemblies replaced by us carry a direct 90-day hardware warranty coverage. This guarantees repair quality and reliability.
Can you repair displays that have cracked screens or liquid damage?
Liquid damage to display connector COF ribbons can be repaired using micro soldering bounds in some cases. If the actual glass screen panel is cracked, the panel must be completely replaced. Panel replacements depend on manufacturing parts availability.
